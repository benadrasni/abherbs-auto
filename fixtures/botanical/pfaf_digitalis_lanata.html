digitalis lanata - Ehrh.

Habitats Woods and scrub.

Range E. Europe.

Physical Characteristics

digitalis lanata is an evergreen Biennial/Perennial growing to 0.6 m (2ft). It is in leaf all year, in flower from June to July, and the seeds ripen in September.

Cultivation details

An easily grown plant, succeeding in ordinary garden soil, especially if it is rich in organic matter.

Related Plants

Lilium canadense habitats Loose fertile soil along woodland edges or in grass and thickets.
